Verification

This page lists, for every component, how it is checked against code written by others: pyribs 0.12.0, QDax 0.5.0, pymoo 0.6.2, MOME-PGX, or the equations of the paper. Where the result differs, the table says why. Items that need more work link to an issue.

Methods

The table names the strongest check that passes.

MethodWhat is compared
ReferenceThe same scripted inputs go through the other library and through this package, and the outputs agree exactly or to rounding: statuses, improvements, kept solutions, cells, metrics, children. A Python script in test/reference writes the other library's outputs, with the versions in its requirements.txt and the header of the script, and a test compares them on every run of the test suite.
PaperThe update equations of the paper, written independently in NumPy (test/reference/generate_strategies.py), run on fixed inputs. Used where a library departs from the paper.
DistributionFor an operator that draws random numbers, which no two libraries draw alike: the mean, standard deviation, quantiles and correlation of 200 000 children of fixed parents.
Whole runA full run in two libraries with the same settings, in benchmark/compare; coverage and QD score agree within the spread between seeds. This catches gross errors, not small ones.
InternalTwo paths of this package agree: device and host, ForwardDiff and closed-form gradients.

Archives

ComponentCompared withMethodResult
GridArchivepyribs GridArchive, QDax MapElitesRepertoireReferenceStatuses, improvements, cells and kept solutions after every batch equal pyribs; kept solutions equal QDax.
CVTArchivepyribs CVTArchive, QDax get_cells_indicesReferenceThe same cell for every descriptor (pyribs clips descriptors to the bounds first, QDax does not). The k-means centroids are compared by their mean squared distance to 1 000 000 uniform points (100 000 samples, 3 seeds): 1.709e-4 for 1 000 cells in two dimensions, against 1.694e-4 for QDax and 1.725e-4 for pyribs; 1.902e-5, 1.856e-5 and 1.975e-5 for 10 000 cells; 5.08e-2, 5.06e-2 and 5.06e-2 for 1 000 cells in six dimensions. QDax's scikit-learn KMeans seeds with greedy k-means++, which explains its lead: scikit-learn with plain k-means++, this package's default, gives 1.900e-5 for 10 000 cells. Its longer iterations explain the six-dimensional gap. pyribs seeds at random samples. GreedyKMeansPlusPlus gives the greedy seeding.
CategoricalArchivepyribs CategoricalArchive (QDax has none)ReferenceEqual.
SlidingBoundariesArchivepyribs SlidingBoundariesArchive (QDax has none)ReferenceEqual up to fitness ties and pyribs's tolerance of 1e-6 below a boundary. pyribs 0.12.0 clips descriptors to the old bounds while it re-adds solutions during a remap; the reference corrects this.
ThresholdArchivepyribs GridArchive with learning_rateReferenceEqual.
UnstructuredArchivepyribs ProximityArchive (one neighbor, local competition); QDax UnstructuredRepertoireReference (pyribs)Equal to pyribs, one solution at a time. QDax 0.5.0 computes wrong nearest-neighbour distances (an extra axis on the mask of empty slots), so of 150 random solutions it keeps 108–124, hundreds of pairs closer than its threshold; reported as QDax#247, with a fix proposed in QDax#248. With that line corrected it keeps 25–27, no two closer than the threshold, and the rest of the difference is by design: QDax rejects a solution whose second-nearest neighbour is also within the threshold, and decides a batch jointly, dropping a solution if a fitter one of the same batch is within the threshold. A plain implementation of these two rules gives the same solutions as corrected QDax.
DNSArchivepyribs DNSArchive, QDax DominatedNoveltyRepertoireReferenceEqual to both.
DensityArchivepyribs DensityArchive (QDax has none)ReferenceEqual densities. pyribs's reservoir sampling replaces the wrong elements when it replaces several in one batch; this package follows Li (1994). See DDS under Algorithms.
ParetoGridArchiveQDax MOMERepertoire, MOME-PGX, pymooReferenceEqual fronts and hypervolumes. By default, solutions with equal fitness vectors are not stored twice (RejectDuplicates); KeepDuplicates gives QDax's behavior.
SpreadArchiveQDax MELSRepertoireReferenceEqual. When several solutions of one batch pass against the elite of a cell, QDax writes them all to the cell in one scatter, and an arbitrary one of them remains; this package keeps the one that adding them one at a time keeps.
GradientArchivethrough OMG-MEGA, belowReferenceEqual.
DeviceGridArchive, DeviceCVTArchivethe host archivesInternalThe same cells as on the host, ties included.

Emitters and evolution strategies

ComponentCompared withMethodResult
VariationEmitterpyribs, QDaxWhole runMAP-Elites on Rastrigin agrees with both at every batch size.
CMAEmitter: rankers, selection, restartspyribs EvolutionStrategyEmitterReferenceStatuses, ranking keys, parent counts and restarts equal for six rankers, both selection rules and three restart rules. Candidates with equal keys are ordered differently: this package puts the earlier one first, pyribs the later one (and, for the objective ranker, an arbitrary one).
CMAESthe CMA-ES tutorial (Hansen, 2016)PaperEqual. pyribs's CMA-ES departs from the tutorial in five places: the rank-one weight c1², an evolution path p_c not divided by σ, a step-size update (‖p_σ‖²/n − 1)/2, another h_σ test, and a lazily updated eigendecomposition.
PyribsCMAESpyribs CMAEvolutionStrategyReferenceEqual mean, step size, covariance and paths to 1e-15 over a short run and a 20-generation run, with the eigendecomposition recomputed in the same generations. It copies all five differences; see PyribsCMA.
SepCMAESRos and Hansen (2008) with pycma's constantsPaperEqual. pyribs's sep-CMA-ES departs from pycma in six places, which account for the whole difference: with all six undone, a copy of pyribs equals this package to 2e-16. Its rank-one rate is c1 · conedf where pycma has conedf, applied twice, which makes it 10⁶–10¹⁴ times smaller for 20 to 1 000 dimensions; its rank-μ offset is 0 where pycma has 0.25; and it shares the step-size rule, the rank-one weight and the unscaled path p_c of its CMA-ES. The h_σ test changes nothing in these runs. Asked upstream: pyribs#731.
LMMAESthe paper; pyribs LMMAEvolutionStrategyReferenceEqual to both.
OpenAIESthe paper; pyribs OpenAIEvolutionStrategyReferenceEqual to both.
BanditSchedulerpyribs BanditSchedulerReferenceThe same choice in all 230 rounds without a tie.
GradientArborescenceEmitterpyribs GradientArborescenceEmitterReferenceEqual branches, rankings, parent counts and points, with Adam and with gradient ascent.
GradientOperatorEmitterpyribs GradientOperatorEmitter, QDax OMGMEGAEmitterReferenceEqual children for the same parents and coefficients. pyribs also adds Gaussian noise to every parent before the gradient step (its sigma), which the paper and QDax do not; QDax's sigma_g is a variance.
DeviceVariationEmitter, DeviceCMAEmitterQDax on a GPU; the host emittersWhole run, InternalAgree; the GPU runs are to be repeated with the current version: #85.

Algorithms

A whole run per algorithm against the library that has it, with the settings of both set equal.

AlgorithmCompared withResult
MAPElitespyribs, QDaxAgree.
CMAMEpyribs, QDaxAgree with pyribs for improvement, optimizing and random direction emitters. QDax recombines and restarts differently and reaches other coverage.
CMAMAEpyribsLower coverage than pyribs, from pyribs's covariance update; with strategy=PyribsCMAES it agrees.
CMAMEGApyribsAgree.
MOMEQDaxAgree, with equal global hypervolume.
MELSQDaxAgree. On noisy 10-dimensional Rastrigin over 20 seeds, coverage 0.283 ± 0.023 against 0.291 ± 0.021 and best fitness −68.1 ± 10.4 against −69.5 ± 8.6, differences of 1.1 and 0.5 standard errors. QDax's rule for several solutions entering one cell makes no measurable difference: with it, this package reaches 0.281 and −69.5.
DDSpyribsWith strategy=PyribsCMAES, agrees with pyribs once pyribs's reservoir sampling is corrected (coverage 0.89–0.90 against 0.87–0.92 on 20-dimensional Rastrigin); pyribs as released reaches 0.76–0.80. With the default CMAES, searches that leave the box of Rastrigin seldom restart (coverage 0.66–0.70); with the box as lower and upper, 0.90–0.92. pyribs also restarts every search after its first round, in which the buffer is empty and every batch has density zero.

The default settings of the algorithm constructors follow pyribs's emitter defaults, not always those of the papers; the docstring of each constructor gives the settings of its paper.
